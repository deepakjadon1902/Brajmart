The invoice layout and company details follow the user-supplied
`invoice-single-inc.pdf`. The logo is a high-resolution crop from that original.
No sample customer data is part of the production template.

The Noto Sans and Noto Sans Devanagari fonts are distributed under the included
SIL Open Font License. They provide embedded Latin, Hindi, and rupee glyphs.

Keep this directory alongside `dist` when deploying the backend. Paths resolve
from both `src/lib` and `dist/lib` without depending on the working directory.
